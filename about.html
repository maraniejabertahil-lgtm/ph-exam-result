About PH EXAM RESULT

Who We Are

Welcome to PH EXAM RESULT, an independent online directory designed to help Filipino examinees, students, and professionals easily access and check official government examination results in the Philippines.

Our Scope: PRC & CSC Exams

Passing a board or civil service exam is a massive milestone. Our platform is built to provide a fast, clean, and mobile-friendly portal where examinees and their families can quickly check results without navigating heavy or slow government websites during peak traffic hours. We currently feature:

  • PRC Licensure Examinations: Alphabetical lists of passers, board passing rates, topnotchers, and school performance summaries for professional boards (Nurses, Teachers, Engineers, Midwives, and more).
  • CSC Civil Service Examinations (Coming Soon): Career Service Examination - Professional and Sub-Professional eligibility results for government service hopefuls.

Important Disclaimer

We are not affiliated, associated, authorized, endorsed by, or in any way officially connected with the Professional Regulation Commission (PRC), the Civil Service Commission (CSC), or any other government agency. All official exam results, statistics, and notices belong solely to their respective government bodies.

Visitors are always advised to verify their official ratings, check certificate issuance schedules, and complete registration requirements directly through official government portals such as online.prc.gov.ph or the official CSC website.
